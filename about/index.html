Who is b

Barry Floore is a writer, composer, and independent producer in Jacksonville, Florida. On book covers and credit blocks the byline is b. floore.

The stage work on this site is SLOP! The Musical, a one-act comedy about a Miami painter and the machines that want her style; Geoff & Maxie, a parody about an island, its monsters, and everyone who looked away; and spun/calliope, still in the lab.

Benny: A Family Tragedy is a novella about Roy, a freelance writer who builds a small assistant because the house is quiet. The Lying Liars is a podcast of stories about love, deception, and the lies people tell right before they leave. For younger readers there is FRIENDLY ROBOTS!, picture books and coloring adventures, six books and counting.

Day work is in AI, after urban planning, close to two decades of emergency and critical-care nursing, and later work with language models. The essays, the musicals, and the fiction sit alongside that. Cultivating Ethical AI is the podcast in the writing feed.

If it carries the Floore name, it started here.
