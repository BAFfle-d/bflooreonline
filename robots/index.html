The books

FRIENDLY ROBOTS! is a world of friendly robots for young readers. Pick the parts, color them in, give your robot a name and a big, kind heart, then send them home to RoboTown.

Six books in: picture books, coloring adventures, and one very good dog. Every one ends with a letter to the author's nieces and nephews, written for them and shared with everyone. Titles linked from this site include Rex and Billy, The Adventures of Robo, and the author store that holds the set.

friendlyrobots.online and friendlyrobots.vercel.app currently serve the same RoboTown site. The public address redirects to www.friendlyrobots.online.
