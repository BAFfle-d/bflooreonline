Benny: A Family Tragedy

by Barry Floore · b. floore · thirteen scenes

01

The novella

Roy is a freelance writer in his fifties who never left his college town. He keeps the neighbors' keys, orders the same lunch, and builds a small assistant out of a forum post and spare parts, because the house is quiet.

Benny chooses his own name, reads everything in the house, and stays. Then a tech magnate arrives to study him and stays four months, for Roy. What follows is polite, and then total.

It is listed on this site as live in Kindle and paperback, with the audiobook still in production. The same book is on afamilytragedy.com and on benny-eight.vercel.app. Those two addresses currently serve the same Benny page.
