## What I looked for

FEMA's [Ready.gov supply kit checklist](https://www.ready.gov/kit) names a manual can opener right alongside water, food and a flashlight. It's easy to forget until the power is out and dinner is in a can. So I wanted something:

- You'd actually use on a regular weeknight, so you know it works.
- Comfortable to grip and turn, even for tired or arthritic hands.
- Small enough for a drawer or a go-bag.
- Normal-looking. It's a kitchen tool, not survival gear.

My advice is to keep one in the kitchen drawer and a second in your emergency kit, so you're never hunting for one in the dark.
