A wallet is the one piece of gear most people already carry every day. That makes it a good place to keep a few backup basics: a copy of your ID, an emergency contact card, a little cash and the cards you would need if you had to leave home in a hurry. [Ready.gov's kit list](https://www.ready.gov/kit) suggests keeping cash and copies of important documents on hand, and a slim wallet you already trust is an easy way to do that.

RFID blocking is a small extra. Many contactless cards can be read at close range, and a blocking lining adds a layer of privacy for the cards you carry. It is a sensible feature to have, and it costs very little to include.

## Make your wallet part of your plan

Whichever wallet you carry, a few small additions make it more useful on an ordinary day and on a hard one:

- A photocopy or photo printout of your ID and insurance card
- A short list of emergency contacts and key phone numbers on paper
- A small amount of cash in small bills
- Any medication or allergy notes you would want someone to know

Set it up once and then go about your day.
