SLOP! The Musical

by Barry Floore · b. floore · script and score

01

The show

Mia is an MFA-trained painter in Miami when someone offers to turn her unmistakable style into an automated product empire. The question is not whether the machines can paint. It is whether her hand, and her choice, still belong in anything that carries her name.

SLOP! is a pop-forward comedy about authorship, labor, friendship, and what it means to make something with machines. It was knowingly co-written with the models it puts onstage: they are in the cast list, and they get lines.

Now playing: "Prompt Me, Baby, One More Time!" The note on this site mentions a goldfish narrator, a missing operatic llama, and one imperious cat.

slopthemusical.com and slopthemusical.vercel.app currently serve the same show page.
